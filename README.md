# p(doom)

**The apocalypse has a dependency graph.** [Enter the Worldwright expedition](https://aaron-alderman.github.io/p-doom/), pick a claim, and bring back a proof, a clean kill, or a test that could separate two explanations.

The expedition includes three illustrated dispatches and a board of 240 propositions from the Yudkowsky–MIRI reconstruction. The field notebook turns your work into a source-pinned review packet. Prepare a public submission, inspect the issue draft, and post it to the [review queue](https://github.com/aaron-alderman/p-doom/issues) from your GitHub account.

Read the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for what counts as a proof or a clean kill. A review disposition applies to the submitted proposition and its stated scope. The [dossier register](corpus/programmes/ai-alignment-risk/yudkowsky_miri_algebra_v1.json) controls the underlying research claims.

The source and local replay commands are in the [Encounter app](applications/pdoom-encounter/README.md). GitHub Pages compiles that source and runs its checks before deploying.
