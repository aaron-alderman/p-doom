# p(doom) illustration prompts

The built-in image generation tool created these four imagined scenes for the Worldwright expedition. The published JPEGs preserve the selected compositions. They are editorial illustrations.

**[Observatory](observatory.jpg)**

> Create a cinematic high quality wide landscape editorial illustration for a speculative research expedition website called p(doom). Imagined remote mountain observatory on a rocky ridge at blue hour, massive crater basin disappearing into the distance, a tiny group of field researchers with warm lanterns gathered around an open map by the observatory on the RIGHT half, a monumental faint segmented eclipse ring over the mountains. Left half quiet dark indigo atmospheric negative space for web typography separately overlaid. Sophisticated dark ink and painted gouache textures, subtle grain, chiaroscuro, plum/navy, amber/coral embers, warm cream, tiny olive instrumentation glints. Grounded, adult, exploratory, dry intellectual tension, not horror gore or sensational robot apocalypse. Stunning coherent composition with focal right and broad landscape, landscape 16:9 or wider. No lettering, no glyphs, no words, no watermark.

**[The off switch](off-switch.jpg)**

> Create a wide landscape original editorial illustration for a rationalist research expedition article about corrigibility. In an imagined remote observatory workshop, an old brass mechanical off-switch lever with a single amber indicator in foreground, one human technician's gloved hand hovering, quiet precision equipment and indigo shadows behind. Slight visual wit: the mundane lever feels more consequential than the enormous machine in the distance. Sophisticated painted gouache and dark ink, tactile paper grain, fine edges, chiaroscuro, plum/navy and warm amber, cinematic depth, restrained. Landscape frame with strong centered subject, no lettering or numbers, no words, no watermark.

**[The passing grade](passing-grade.jpg)**

> Create a wide landscape original editorial illustration for a speculative science field journal article about passing AI evaluations. An imagined observatory testing bench, two identical small antique optical testing instruments with identical pale green indicator lights, their differently routed exposed cables revealed in crisp pools of amber light underneath the bench. One dark glass surface reflects both instruments, slight visual wit from their matching faces and divergent wiring. Sophisticated dark ink and hand-painted gouache, tactile fine paper grain, coherent cinema composition, plum/navy with amber and sage, adult science magazine. Landscape with balanced, centered key objects, no lettering or numbers, no words, no watermark.

**[The last link](last-link.jpg)**

> Create a wide original landscape editorial illustration for a speculative science field journal article about combining several individually plausible premises into one actual route. Wide panoramic aerial of a dramatic mountain valley at night, warm lantern trail markers trace two routes through the mountains, a visible uncrossed chasm cuts across their apparent meeting point. Small human scale, analytical visual metaphor, sophisticated dark ink and gouache with paper grain, blue/plum mountain shadows, restrained amber lamps and dawn coral at horizon. Beautiful, stark and readable at thumbnail scale, landscape 16:9. No lettering, no words, no map labels, no watermark.
